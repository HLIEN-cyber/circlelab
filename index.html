Apollonius Circle Lab（阿波羅尼斯圓）

All points P with PA : PB = m : n form a circle (m ≠ n). Drag A, B, or P. A and B snap to grid points.

Ratio 比值

PA : PB = 1 : 2

Locus of P 軌跡方程式

Check with P 驗證

P PA PB PA / PB

Why is it a circle? 為什麼是圓？

  1. 1
    Square the ratio 比值平方
  2. 2
    Expand 展開

  3. 3
    Find a diameter 找直徑

    The circle crosses line AB at the internal and external division points (內分點、外分點) of AB in the ratio m : n. They are the two ends of a diameter.

Try 試試看

Think 想一想

  1. Change the ratio to 1 : 3, 1 : 4, 1 : 5. Does the circle get bigger or smaller? Which point does it shrink toward?
  2. Which point, A or B, is inside the circle? Why?
  3. What happens when m : n gets close to 1 : 1? Compare with “Set 1 : 1”.
  4. Is the center between A and B, or outside segment AB?
